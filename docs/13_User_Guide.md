# User guide

This guide is written for a parent, teacher, or anyone handing the app to a
child — no technical knowledge required.

## What you need

- A smartphone with a camera and a web browser (Chrome, Safari, etc.).
- The web address (URL) for the app.
- A printed set of flashcards from one of the app's categories — for
  example, Animals, Alphabets, Numbers, or Shapes. Ask your content
  maintainer where to get/print these if you don't have them (see
  [14_Admin_Guide.md](14_Admin_Guide.md) if you're the one making them).

## Opening the app

1. Open the web address in your phone's browser.
2. The browser may ask you to accept a security certificate the first time
   (this is expected on some setups — see
   [11_Troubleshooting.md](11_Troubleshooting.md) if it looks unusual).
3. You'll see a home screen with a grid of picture tiles — one per category
   of cards, each showing a sample picture, its name, and how many cards it
   has.

## Finding a category

- Scroll the grid, or use the search bar at the top to type part of a
  category name (like "anim") or a card's name (like "lion") — matching
  categories appear instantly as you type.
- A category with a lot of cards is marked "Large Deck." That's just a
  heads-up that scanning might feel a little slower in a big set — nothing
  is broken.

## Scanning a card

1. Tap a category tile.
2. Your browser will ask for camera permission — tap **Allow**. (If you
   don't see this prompt, or you tap "Don't Allow" by mistake, see
   [11_Troubleshooting.md](11_Troubleshooting.md) for how to fix it.)
3. Point the camera at one printed flashcard from that category.

**Tips for a good scan:**

- Use good, even light — avoid deep shadows or strong glare on the card.
- Keep the card flat, not bent or curled.
- Fill most of the camera view with the one card.
- Hold the phone reasonably steady for a second while it locks on.

4. When the card is recognized, a 3D model of the picture appears standing
   on the card, and the app says the word out loud.

## Playing with the model

Once a model appears, you can:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Drag with one finger | Spins the model around, like a turntable. |
| Pinch with two fingers | Grows or shrinks the model. |
| Press and hold, then drag | Slides the model around on the card. |
| Tap the model | Says the word again. |
| Tap the speaker button (🔊, bottom left) | Also says the word again — use this any time, even without touching the model. |
| Tap **Reset** (bottom right) | Puts the model back to its original size, angle, and position. |

There's also a **?** button in the top-right corner that opens a quick
picture-based reminder of these same gestures.

## Leaving a model in the room ("Place Here")

Once a model is on screen, you can tap **Place Here** to leave it standing
in the room instead of on the card — put the card away and the model stays
put, so you can walk around and look at it from different angles.

- **Turning your phone works great** — spin around in place and the model
  stays anchored where you left it.
- **Walking a long distance will make it drift** — this app doesn't have
  full room-mapping AR, so it's a "look around" trick, not a "walk all the
  way across the room" trick. If it looks off after walking, just tap
  **Return to Card** and place it again from where you're standing.
- Tap **Return to Card** any time to put the model back exactly where it
  was.

## Going back / switching categories

Tap **Back** (top-left) at any time to stop the camera and return to the
category grid, where you can pick a different topic.

## Things that are expected, not broken

- **The word doesn't play the very first time you open the app that
  session.** This is a phone security rule about sound — just tap the
  speaker button once, and every card after that should play normally.
- **A card doesn't get recognized.** Try better lighting, hold the card
  flatter, and fill more of the camera frame with it. Some very plain
  cards (solid colors, no texture) are genuinely harder for any phone
  camera to recognize — see
  [12_Known_Issues_and_Limitations.md](12_Known_Issues_and_Limitations.md).
- **The room-pinned model looks slightly off after walking around.** See
  "Leaving a model in the room" above — this is a known, accepted limit of
  how the feature works.

If something looks genuinely broken (not one of the above), see
[11_Troubleshooting.md](11_Troubleshooting.md), or contact whoever manages
the app for you.
